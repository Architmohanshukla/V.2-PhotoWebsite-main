✨ HOW TO USE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

UPLOADING PHOTOS (WEB INTERFACE):
  1. Click "+ Upload Photos" button in gallery
  2. Drag images into upload area OR click to select
  3. Choose category (auto-detects from filename)
  4. Click "Upload Photos"
  5. Follow on-screen instructions
